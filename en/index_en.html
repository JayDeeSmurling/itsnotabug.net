It’s Not a Bug, It’s a Feature.

It's official. The domain is registered, the server is up and running, and the first git commit has reached production. Welcome to itsnotabug.eu.

Why this name? If you've been in IT for more than 15 minutes, you definitely know this phrase. It's the ultimate developer's excuse when the system begins acting... creatively. But to me, it's more than just an industry joke.

In the world of code (and life), the line between a bug and a feature can be remarkably thin. Often, it's errors that lead us to the biggest breakthroughs, force us to rethink our assumptions, and teach respect for documentation. On this blog, I share insights on:

  • Technologies that sometimes fail us (yet we love them anyway).
  • Solving problems that shouldn't exist in the first place.
  • System architecture designed so that "bugs" simply happen less often.

Make yourself at home. I hope you find something useful here for your next debugging session.
